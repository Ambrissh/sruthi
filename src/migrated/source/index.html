Welcome!          
Shruthi Swara Laya, USA, is a fine arts institution dedicated to Performing Arts in Indian Music and Dance. It was founded and established by Anuradha Suresh in April 1998 in Fremont, California. We offer classes currently in Carnatic Vocal music and Harikatha in Fremont. We hope that you, as a reader, will benefit from the information we have provided here about Shruthi Swara Laya. We thank you for stopping by our website and hope that you can join us in our mission to promote the varied and unique arts of India.
